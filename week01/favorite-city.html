My Favorite City

Tokyo, Japan

Tokyo is a vibrant capital city of Japan, known for mixing ultra-modern skyscrapers with historic Shinto shrines. It is a massive global hub for technology, fashion, delicious cuisine, and pop culture.

Some of the most famous attractions include the bustling Shibuya Crossing, the historic Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa, and the breathtaking views from the Tokyo Skytree.
